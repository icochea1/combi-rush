Room in the back!

Race a decorated Peruvian combi through Lima, the Costa Verde, the Panamericana, the Andes and Iquitos. Jump over traffic, grab passengers, hit turbo and challenge your friends on the exact same road.

How to play

  • Swipe left / right (or tap a side of the screen) to change lanes.
  • Swipe up to jump over cars, mototaxis and obstacles. Ramps launch you even higher.
  • Drive in the outer lane past a bus stop to pick up passengers — they add points and seconds.
  • Fill the meter and swipe down (or tap the button) for turbo: plough through traffic for a few seconds.
  • Each bus stop (every 1,500 m) adds time. When the clock hits zero, the run ends.

Ranking and challenges

Pick a driver name to join the weekly and all-time rankings — no email or password. After a run, tap Challenge a friend to share a code. Your friend races the same road with the same traffic, and the challenge page shows who won.

Help

Report a name: in the ranking, tap ⋮ next to a player and choose Report name or Block player.

Delete your account: Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your driver name, scores and challenges from our server immediately.

Contact: icochea1@gmail.com — we usually reply within 2 business days.

En español

Maneja una combi chicha por Lima, la Costa Verde, la Panamericana, los Andes e Iquitos. Salta carros, recoge pasajeros, activa el turbo y reta a tus amigos en la misma pista.

  • Desliza a la izquierda o derecha (o toca un lado de la pantalla) para cambiar de carril.
  • Desliza hacia arriba para saltar carros, mototaxis y obstáculos. Las rampas te lanzan más alto.
  • Pasa por el carril de afuera junto al paradero para recoger pasajeros: suman puntos y segundos.
  • Llena la barra y desliza hacia abajo (o toca el botón) para el turbo "¡PISA!".
  • Cada paradero (cada 1.500 m) te da más tiempo.

Reportar un nombre: en el ranking, toca ⋮ junto al jugador y elige Reportar nombre o Bloquear jugador.

Eliminar tu cuenta: Ajustes → Eliminar mi cuenta. Se borran al instante tu nombre de chofer, puntajes y retos de nuestro servidor.
